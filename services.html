Open Auto Transport

Open auto shipping is the standard freight method for sedans, compacts, and crossovers across the United States. Vehicles are safely secured on two-tier multi-car trailers.

Enclosed Auto Transport

Enclosed shipping seals your vehicle inside hard-sided or soft-sided trailers, shielding it from rain, sun, road gravel, and salt. Recommended for luxury, exotic, and antique cars.

  • Complete protection from all elements
  • Low-angle ramps and hydraulic liftgates
  • High cargo insurance coverage policies

Comparison Guide

Open vs. Enclosed Transport: Which Is Right For You?

Selecting the best trailer type depends on your vehicle's value, road clearance, and personal protection preference.

Feature / Factor 🚛 Open Auto Transport 🛡️ Enclosed Auto Transport
Primary Advantage Cost-effective, highly available carrier capacity Maximum protection against all weather and debris
Best For Everyday sedans, family SUVs, leased vehicles Classics, luxury exotics, custom paint, vintage
Trailer Design Open-air, 2-level steel framework Fully covered solid-wall or hard-shell enclosure
Loading Mechanism Standard angled vehicle ramps Hydraulic liftgate or extended low-angle ramps
Carrier Availability Broad daily nationwide routes (90% of US haulers) Specialized dedicated enclosed carriers
Average Cost Difference Standard market base rate Typically 35%–50% premium over open carrier
