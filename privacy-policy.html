Privacy Policy

How AutoMove United collects, uses, and protects your information.

Home / Privacy Policy

AutoMove United is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website (automoveunited.com) or interact with us via phone, email, or digital forms.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, request an auto transport quote, phone, or email, we may collect personal information that you provide, including:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number(s)
  • Email Address
  • Vehicle Details (Year, Make, Model, Condition)
  • Origin & Destination Locations (ZIP codes, City, State)
  • Desired shipping dates and logistical notes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively for legitimate vehicle shipping business purposes, including:

  • Calculating and delivering customized auto transport quotes
  • Communicating with you via emails, phone calls, and text messages (SMS) regarding your quote request, follow-ups, and shipment coordination
  • Facilitating pickup, transit updates, and delivery confirmations with assigned motor carriers
  • Providing responsive customer support and administrative updates

3. Strict Mobile Data & SMS Sharing Prohibition

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.

4. SMS Communications & Opt-Out Rights

If you provide consent to receive SMS communications from AutoMove United, you may receive messages concerning your quote, carrier scheduling, and delivery status. Message and data rates may apply depending on your cellular provider plan. Message frequency will vary. You may revoke SMS consent at any time by replying "STOP" to any text message, or reply "HELP" for customer assistance.

5. Information Security

AutoMove United employs industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards to protect customer personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration.
