PRICING FACTORS

What Affects Your Car Shipping Cost?

Several factors can influence your auto transport quote. Here's what to keep in mind:

Get My Quote →

Pickup & Delivery Locations

Distance and route between locations.

Total mileage is the primary baseline for your shipping quote. Popular interstate lanes between major metropolitan hubs benefit from high carrier volume and lower per-mile rates compared to remote or rural routes requiring route deviations.

Vehicle Size, Weight & Type

Larger and heavier vehicles may cost more to transport.

Larger vehicles such as full-size SUVs, heavy pickup trucks, and extended vans occupy more space and take up greater trailer weight allowance than compact sedans, directly affecting transport capacity on multi-car carriers.

Open vs. Enclosed Transport

Enclosed offers more protection, open is more affordable.

Open auto transport is the standard, most economical choice chosen for over 90% of shipments. Enclosed transport provides complete weather and road debris shielding for classic, high-end luxury, and exotic vehicles.

Seasonal Demand & Market Conditions

Demand can affect availability and pricing.

Market rates reflect national supply and demand patterns, including annual snowbird migration (North to South in autumn, reverse in spring), summer peak relocations, and winter storm route adjustments in northern states.

Vehicle Operability

Running or non-running vehicles require special handling.

Vehicles that start, steer, and brake roll safely onto carrier trailers. Non-running or inoperable automobiles require winching equipment, specialized loading procedures, and additional carrier handling time.

Requested Timing

Specific pickup or delivery dates may impact availability.

Standard scheduling with flexible dispatch windows allows us to coordinate the most competitive carrier rate along your route. Expedited or urgent pickup requirements necessitate prioritized dispatch and dedicated space.

Carrier Availability

Current carrier capacity and route demand.

Carrier route schedules and available open spots fluctuate dynamically. Providing flexible pickup windows allows our coordinators to match your vehicle with verified haulers passing directly through your route.
