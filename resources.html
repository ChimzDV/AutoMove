Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Shipping Questions

Immediate answers to the most common questions vehicle owners ask before booking auto transport.

Open trailer transport is the standard industry method, chosen by over 90% of vehicle owners due to its cost efficiency and frequent route availability. Enclosed shipping costs approximately 40% to 60% more and provides complete shielding against road debris, weather, and grime, making it ideal for high-value exotics, classic collector vehicles, and antique restorations.

We recommend booking 1 to 2 weeks prior to your preferred pickup date. This provides our dispatch team sufficient lead time to identify vetted, insured carriers traveling along your exact corridor and secure the most competitive rate. If you require expedited pickup within 24 to 48 hours, expedited dispatch services are available.

At AutoMove United, we provide transparent, realistic estimates with zero hidden fees, terminal charges, or sudden spikes, delivering competitive pricing without any runaround. Rates are based on verified current carrier market conditions, vehicle specifications, operable status, and corridor distance.

You or an authorized adult representative (18+) must be present at both pickup and delivery to complete and sign the Bill of Lading (BOL) inspection report. You only need your vehicle keys; registration and title stay with you and do not need to be left with the driver for interstate transport.
